# Where a source-to-FHIR laboratory benchmark would help

Research date: 24 November 2026.

An integration engineer receives two FHIR Observations for adjacent rows of a laboratory report. Both codes exist, both numbers are valid decimals, and both resources pass structural validation. Did each number reach the right test? A benchmark can answer that question when it has independent expected answers for the printed rows. Build around this use case. Give vendors the same input, allow any extractor or converter to produce FHIR, and show reviewers which literal, row and resource disagree. A synthetic test proves behavior for that input. Estimating error rates on routine care requires a separately designed evaluation.

## What implementers and public bodies can use

**European Union.** The EHDS Regulation entered into force on 26 March 2025. The Commission describes March 2027 as the deadline for several implementing acts, March 2029 as the first stage for exchanging priority data such as patient summaries and electronic prescriptions, and March 2031 as the stage for the group including laboratory results, medical images and discharge reports. A buyer can request a reproducible demonstration against an identified guide today. A passing benchmark alone cannot establish legal conformity, institutional endorsement or comparability across national requirements. [Commission timeline](https://health.ec.europa.eu/ehealth-digital-health-and-care/european-health-data-space-regulation-ehds_en) · [Regulation (EU) 2025/327](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2025/327/oj/eng)

**United States.** The federal certification program lists US Core 6.1.0 among the base standards for the standardized API criterion and provides a dedicated Inferno test kit for § 170.315(g)(10). The Standards Version Advancement Process provides a route to approved newer versions. A document-fixture result can supplement integration QA. Certification evidence requires the applicable program criteria, including the API behavior under test. [ONC base standards and test tools](https://healthit.gov/certification-health-it/certification-process/onc-conformance-test-tools/) · [Standards and voluntary updates](https://healthit.gov/certification-health-it/standards-hub/) · [Program test kit](https://fhir.healthit.gov/test-kits/onc-certification-g10/)

**Where there are real implementers.** Oracle documents an R4 DiagnosticReport search and explains which report types populate the result references. IHE-Europe's March 2026 Brussels Plugathon included hands-on testing of European implementation guides for laboratory and other primary-use exchanges. These are credible interface and community targets. Neither is evidence that a vendor, regulator or event has adopted this project. [Oracle Health API](https://docs.oracle.com/en/industries/health/millennium-platform-apis/mfrap/op-diagnosticreport-get.html) · [IHE-Europe program](https://connectathon.ihe-europe.net/ihe-plugathon-2026)

## What failed in published studies

| Primary study | Observation and design consequence |
| --- | --- |
| Two PCORnet DataMarts, published 2022, February 2023 journal issue | Of 179,537,986 included quantitative results representing 3,029 tests, the authors estimated a result-weighted LOINC mapping error rate of 4.6%. About half of mapping errors involved the property. The two sites and the quantitative-only selection limit generalization. Include deliberate specimen, method and property mismatches. Keep row counts and distinct-test counts visible. [Original study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9846663/) |
| Lin, Vreeman and Huff, three institutions, 2011 | When sites exchanged LOINC-coded tests, quantitative results still exhibited missing units, unit synonyms and scale differences. Non-quantitative results varied in their use of acronyms, enumerations and free text. A code-only score misses these failures. Add qualitative and absent-result cases with their own denominators. [Original study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22195138/) |
| Löbe and colleagues, German Medical Informatics Initiative, 2023 | In their NT-proBNP use case, missing codes in the FHIR search omitted sites' measurements. The authors described seven eligible codes and nine unit representations, and highlighted inequality comparators on measured values. Exercise missing comparators, equivalent units and multiple eligible codes explicitly. Similar or dimensionally compatible codes still require the correct specimen and measurement meaning. [Original paper](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/pdf/10.3233/SHTI230706) |
| Kruse and colleagues, German LADR network, 2024 | Investigators transformed 20 anonymized complete-blood-count messages into two profiles. All 20 European transformations encountered a structural validation problem that they traced to a required extension missing from the package used then. This gives a practical reason to include a known-good upstream control and the package version in every run. The finding concerned a historical package. [Original study](https://medinform.jmir.org/2024/1/e57569) |
| Muñoz Monjas and colleagues, 2025, and an oncology study, 2026 | The TriNetX study reported greater than 90% coverage of lab data with UCUM units after harmonization. The independent oncology study evaluated 428 LOINC codes across about 6.34 billion records. Unit assignments meeting the authors' range-table criterion increased from 73.1% to 99.7%. The oncology work was sponsored by the data provider, and the underlying datasets are restricted. Coverage and agreement with a table measure different things from recovery of a printed value. Preserve the input literal and justify each accepted conversion. [TriNetX study](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijmedinf.2024.105665) · [Oncology study and limitations](https://medinform.jmir.org/2026/1/e81254/) |

## What earns adoption

1. **An integration team can reproduce a meaningful failure.** Supply a small, human-inspectable Spanish report, independent expected rows, a matching FHIR output, and an otherwise well-formed output that swaps two values. Show both row IDs, page locations and FHIR paths. Include decimal commas and inequalities such as `<0,5`. Use FHIR Quantity's comparator and numeric value, while retaining the source literal in the test evidence. [FHIR R4 Quantity](https://hl7.org/fhir/R4/datatypes.html#Quantity)
2. **A laboratory specialist can audit an assertion.** For each mapping rule publish the source label, code system and version, specimen, property, timing, scale and method when supplied, evidence, and review state. Allow legitimate ambiguity and retain local codes. A missing contextual fact deserves its own outcome. Reference intervals printed for a synthetic case can test *transcription*. Biological appropriateness needs a separate laboratory review with the relevant population and method. [LOINC term axes](https://loinc.org/kb/users-guide/major-parts-of-a-loinc-term/) · [EU laboratory Observation](https://hl7.eu/fhir/laboratory/2.1.0/StructureDefinition-Observation-resultslab-eu-lab.html)
3. **A vendor or auditor can repeat the test offline.** Include hashes of fixtures, versions of the standards and tools, terminology coverage, warning counts, and a stable machine-readable diff. Attribute every failure to a named test and distinguish structural, terminology and source discrepancies. Keep copyrighted terminology releases, actual patient reports and facility-specific identifiers outside the public fixture pipeline. Review third-party terms before redistributing vocabulary data. [LOINC license](https://loinc.org/license/) · [SNOMED International licensing and access](https://www.snomed.org/get-snomed)
4. **Measure use outside the author’s machine.** Proposed milestones are two independently implemented adapters running unchanged public cases, one external lab informatician reviewing the initial mappings, and one cross-implementation replay of the EU document examples. Publish the guide versions, outputs, discrepancies and outstanding terminology checks. Track source-to-result accuracy, report omissions, extra Observations, ambiguous matches and comparator failures with denominators per template. Count independent reproductions and previously undetected defects alongside downloads. These are proposed acceptance criteria and provide a path to evidence of demand.
